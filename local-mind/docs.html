How to read availability on these pages

Every page says plainly whether something is in a released build, in a TestFlight build, or only on a development branch. These are three different things and the pages do not blur them. A feature described as in development is not available to download, and no date is promised for it.

Likewise, no page quotes a speed figure without naming the model and quantisation, the machine, the storage the weights were read from, the engine and the context length, and saying how it was measured. Where a figure in the app is modelled rather than measured, the page says so.
