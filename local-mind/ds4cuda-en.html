Caveats that travel with the number

End-to-end flips on the prompt type. Cold prompts: ds4.c still wins below ~2149 generated tokens — the cold-prefill cliff eats the decode margin. Repeated prompts (KV reuse): we win 1.3–2.1× at 128 tokens, at every context.

Prefill parity is flag-gated: the 2.32× whole-prompt fix is measured but default-off (not yet a safe default). Decode +10% is shipped.
